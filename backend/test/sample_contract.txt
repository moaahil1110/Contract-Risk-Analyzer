1. DEFINITIONS
"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by either party during the course of business discussions.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years.

3. NON-DISCLOSURE
Each party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose to any third parties.

4. USE OF INFORMATION
Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than evaluating the potential business relationship.

5. RETURN OF INFORMATION
Upon termination of this Agreement, Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information.

6. REMEDIES
Each party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm and the other party is entitled to seek injunctive relief.
